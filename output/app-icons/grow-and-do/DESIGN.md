# Grow and Do: refinement

The user wants to refine the second candidate from the workspace round.
The calendar and large-check directions overemphasize planning. The original
Grow and Do candidate feels cluttered.

Keep the book/notebook and growth character. Reduce the competing visual
elements and integrate action with the main mark.

## Variations

- Reference: the previous Grow and Do, copied without modification.
- B1 Single Leaf: remove one leaf, narrow the notebook, and reduce the page
  check. The action cue stays explicit but becomes secondary.
- B2 Page Flow: the check is suggested by the upper edge of the right page.
  One leaf and two pages form the whole mark, with no interior decoration.
- B3 Leaf Vein: move the action cue into a single leaf as a check-shaped vein.
  The notebook pages remain empty.

All variants use the same moss and ivory palette. No gradients, shadows,
textures, translucency, calendar bindings, or date grids.

The previously retained Knowledge Sprout is unchanged in `../flat/` and
`../workspace/`.

Each SVG has PNG exports at 1024, 256, 64, and 32 pixels. Review `comparison.png`
or `preview.html`.

Regenerate from the repository root:

```sh
node output/app-icons/render.mjs --refine
```
