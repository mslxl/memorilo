## Updated product direction

Memorilo encompasses reading, notes, journals, scheduling, Todos, and learning.
Its icon should communicate a personal workspace for knowledge and everyday
action. Spaced repetition is one capability within that workspace.

The next round retains the original book-and-leaves candidate and explores:

- Grow and Do: the original growth metaphor with a completed action on a page.
- Daybook: a note/calendar silhouette and a completed action.
- Growing Check: a broad growth-and-progress mark.

All new candidates remain flat. Files live in `workspace/`.

## Current exploration

The single-leaf Grow and Do refinements were rejected for poor visual balance.
The `balanced/` round explored paired, mirrored, and rotational forms with a
single visual center, but scheduling became too abstract to recognize.
The `scheduled/` composites were rejected as unclear or cluttered and too
calendar-led. The `organizer/` round uses a personal organizer as one
recognizable object for notes, knowledge, plans, and actions. Its first candidate
is the final selection.
