# Notes and time: integrated forms

The previous abstract round made scheduling invisible. This round requires a
visible scheduling cue in each candidate while retaining reading or note pages.
The previously retained Knowledge Sprout remains unchanged.

## Requirements from the conversation

- Flat style with solid colors.
- Visual balance and a clear central composition.
- Reading and notes remain represented.
- Scheduling must be visibly present, not just described as an abstract meaning.
- Todos are part of the product; one variation explicitly includes completion.
- Avoid isolated feature badges and the rejected single-leaf compositions.

## Candidates

1. Open Agenda: mirrored open pages, paired calendar bindings, header dividers,
   and four date cells. Notes and scheduling share the same silhouette.
2. Calendar Pages: a calendar/notebook frame containing open pages in negative
   space. More explicitly calendar-led.
3. Daily Folio: the same open-planner frame, with note lines on one page and a
   completed task on the other. Most literal coverage of notes, plans, and Todos.
4. Time Notes: a clock ring and symmetrical clock hands enclosing open pages.
   Time management is explicit without a calendar-shaped outer silhouette.

All use the same moss-green and ivory palette so form remains the variable.
No gradients, shadows, textures, or translucent effects.

Geometric centroids are logged to support positioning, followed by visual
inspection of the large and 32/64px previews. This is a positioning diagnostic,
not a claim that perception can be reduced to a centroid.

## Deliverables

Editable SVGs and PNGs at 1024, 256, 64, and 32 pixels.
The review sheet is `comparison.png`; the local gallery is `preview.html`.

Regenerate from the repository root:

```sh
node output/app-icons/render.mjs --schedule
```
